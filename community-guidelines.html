A THOUGHTFUL COMMUNITY

Community Guidelines

Last updated: October 11, 2026

RangManch brings people together through culture, creativity, and everyday stories. Help make it welcoming and safe for everyone.

Respect people

Do not threaten, bully, stalk, or target people with hateful or degrading content. Respect boundaries and do not publish another person’s private information without permission.

Share responsibly

Post only content you created or have permission to share. Do not impersonate a person or organization, mislead people about who you are, or present false claims as verified fact.

Keep the service safe

Do not post illegal content, sexual exploitation, graphic abuse, scams, malware, or content that encourages real-world harm. Do not attempt to access another person’s account or private messages.

Protect cultural context

Share cultural traditions and identities with care. Do not use slurs, stereotypes, or demeaning portrayals of a community, religion, language, region, or caste.

Report concerns

If content or conduct concerns you, send the profile or post details to safety@rangmanch.in. Include links and a short description. We may review reports and remove content or restrict accounts when appropriate.

These guidelines support the Terms of Use.
