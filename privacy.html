Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

RangManch is a community for sharing stories, photos, videos, and conversations rooted in Indian culture. This policy explains what information the service handles and how it is used.

Information you provide

When you register, we process your email address, username, and the profile details you choose to add, such as your display name, biography, and profile photo. We also store posts, stories, comments, likes, follows, and messages you create.

What other people can see

Public accounts show their posts and active stories to other users. Private accounts restrict in-app post and story access to approved followers; profile identity remains searchable. Stories are removed from the app after 24 hours. Media is currently stored in a public media bucket, so anyone with a direct media link may still access that file, including after a story expires or when the account is private.

Direct messages are available to their sender and recipient. Do not send information in a message that you would not want the recipient to keep or share.

How information is used

Information is used to operate accounts, display profiles and feeds, deliver messages, maintain safety, and troubleshoot the service. RangManch uses Supabase for authentication, database, and media storage, and third-party providers for fonts and some fallback profile images.

Retention and your choices

Profile and content information remains until it is changed or removed, subject to backups and technical retention. Stories are filtered by their 24-hour expiration time. To request account or content removal, contact privacy@rangmanch.in.

Children and safety

RangManch is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above identifies the current version.
